## Sharp coast and contrast

Replace loose rounded shoreline boulders with a continuous inherited cliff edge, fractured faces and irregular pointed rock spires, proposed 5–10m high. These are natural rock formations, not stakes or repeated manufactured cones. Mix widths, lean and heights; avoid identical teeth. Keep the same geographic shoreline and bridge opening. The paper-plan teeth are symbolic cliff marks; exact mesh silhouette and heights need 3D blockout. They must not obscure the common security fence or create a landing beach.

The clean expensive presidential lawn, white palace, heroic gold statue and small speech stage directly contradict concrete military hardware, troops and missiles. Do not add shrubbery, crowd seating, press buildings or a rally plaza. The stage is an approved place where Tramduro can stand and speak; it does not specify when he appears, a mandatory kidnapping route or a frequent roaming NPC. Existing ordinary-play representation through Z and closed palace remain; resolve speech-event timing separately.

20–25 military outside: small walking patrols and lone movement. Mostly open territory, rare cover. Preserve clear 3.5m service passages, 1.5m walking corridors, hangar apron and helipad approach; trucks and stage must not obstruct them. Clearances are invisible reservations, never painted roads.

Military vehicles, helicopter, AA and cartoon nuclear missiles are layout props; driving, firing, detection/damage and pursuit rules are not approved here. Final emergence, speech timing and extraction are OPEN. Do not invent evacuation, indoor palace gameplay or a finale tied to the new stage. Numerical heights and placements are proposed blockout defaults.
