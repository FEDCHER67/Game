CONFIRMED: ROUND CYLINDRICAL HAY BALES
Owner supplied codex-clipboard-45c3ac73-f266-4d97-b85d-1d48291e8c47.png and explicitly requested this feature.

Add agricultural rolled hay/straw bales matching the reference silhouette: a short broad cylinder resting on its curved side, with visible wound layers on the circular ends and a coarse fibrous surface.
Do not substitute rectangular bundles, ordinary conical haystacks, wooden barrels or perfectly smooth plastic cylinders.

Placement direction: near communal barn/hay storage and along cultivated field margins, in a few purposeful groups rather than uniformly scattered around homes. Keep entrance, loading and drive-through clearance. They are occasional solid physical obstacles; do not create a bale wall around fields or houses.

Proposed build defaults, not separately approved exact numbers: 4–6 bales, diameter about 1.4–1.8 m, axial length about 1.2–1.5 m. Use modest size/orientation/colour variation within one consistent stylized low-poly treatment. End-face spiral relief should be readable without photographic texture noise or excessive geometry. Muted straw/golden-brown colour; slightly darker aged examples are acceptable.

Whether bales can roll or be pushed is not decided. Their inclusion does not authorize inventing a physics toy subsystem; static collision is a sufficient layout default until this is discussed.

Русская фиксация: обязательно добавить круглые рулоны сена, как на присланном фото. Число, размеры и расположение выше — предложения для сборки. Исходная карта и картинки не изменены.
