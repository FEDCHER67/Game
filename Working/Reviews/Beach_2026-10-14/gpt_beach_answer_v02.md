## 1. Beach corn seller

### C1 — The Corn Minaret

Best pick

Pitch: A pushy beach salesman who looks like a walking monument to boiled corn.

- Hook: An absurdly tall backpack rack packed with giant yellow corn cobs, rising above his head like a tower.
- Silhouette at 40 m: Tall yellow column. Thin body (1.1 scale), narrow shoulders, five oversized cobs clustered vertically. The rack extends roughly 40 cm above his head.
- Face: 65% Happy / 35% Surprised. Wall-eyed grin, one enormous bucktooth, permanently raised eyebrows.
- Palette: Butter yellow (dominant), dirty olive, tan; red accent.
- Hand: One boiled corn cob, dropped on danger. Worn: Corn tower, backpack harness, yellow vendor apron.
- Gag: He drops the cob and panic-runs, but the enormous corn tower remains visible above beach furniture when he hides. When dragged into the van, the tower makes him look like a toppled market stall.
- CIS reference: The deafening Anapa beach cry: «КУКУРУЗА! ГОРЯЧАЯ КУКУРУЗА!»
- Build: Low-medium. One reusable corn mesh ×5, simple rack, harness, apron. Approximately 800–1,200 new triangles.

### C2 — The Human Corn Boiler

Pitch: A sweaty man who has effectively turned himself into a mobile cooking pot.

- Hook: A gigantic aluminium boiler strapped directly to his stomach and chest, with corn sticking out beneath its crooked lid.
- Silhouette at 40 m: Round metal barrel torso on skinny legs. Belly slider maxed; arms hang slightly away from the boiler (60°).
- Face: 80% Happy / 20% Surprised. Greasy moustache, huge vacant eyes, missing front tooth.
- Palette: Dull silver (dominant), mustard yellow, dirty green; bright red accent.
- Hand: Cooking tongs, dropped on danger. Worn: Boiler, fixed lid, chest harness.
- Gag: He drops the tongs and flees while still wearing his boiler. When grabbed, the rigid pot bumps against the van's door frame while his legs flop uselessly.
- CIS reference: The enormous metal pots used by Black Sea beach food vendors.
- Build: Low. Sixteen-sided pot, oversized lid, visible corn cluster, harness. Approximately 600–900 triangles.

### C3 — The Corn Loudspeaker

Pitch: A seller so aggressively loud that his entire body is built around shouting about corn.

- Hook: A monstrous, corn-shaped yellow megaphone mounted over one shoulder, pointing diagonally into the air.
- Silhouette at 40 m: Asymmetric yellow horn, projecting above and beside his head. Skinny body beneath it.
- Face: 40% Happy / 60% Surprised. Mouth painted permanently wide open, tiny teeth, frantic eyes.
- Palette: Yellow (dominant), faded blue, brown; red accent.
- Hand: Corn cob, dropped on danger. Worn: Oversized megaphone and shoulder harness.
- Gag: He tries to hide behind a beach lounger, but the horn sticks out. After being knocked out, the megaphone still points upward from his ragdoll.
- CIS reference: Beach hawkers screaming their advertisements at the top of their lungs.
- Build: Low. One oversized horn mesh with corn-kernel detailing, harness. Approximately 400–700 triangles.

## 2. Dumb sand-castle builder

### S1 — The Walking Fortress

Pitch: A grown man so proud of his sandcastle that he wears the mould as clothing.

- Hook: A gigantic lime-green plastic sandcastle mould worn around the waist like a fortress, complete with four squat turrets and battlements.
- Silhouette at 40 m: Low, square, crenellated block. Body scale 0.9, large belly, short-looking legs beneath the castle. Wide at the hips rather than shoulders.
- Face: 75% Surprised / 25% Happy. Crossed eyes, two protruding teeth, sand-coloured freckles painted on his cheeks.
- Palette: Lime green (dominant), sand beige, dark navy; orange accent.
- Hand: Tiny orange plastic shovel, dropped on danger. Worn: Huge castle mould, retaining belt, green shirt.
- Gag: A complete idiot in a plastic fortress is suddenly running away from a tiny threat. When knocked out and dragged past his actual sandcastles, he looks like somebody stealing an oversized sandbox toy.
- CIS reference: «Прораб песочницы» — an adult treating a beach sandcastle like the construction project of the century.
- Build: Low-medium. One hollow low-poly castle mould, four simple turrets, belt. Approximately 500–1,000 triangles.

### S2 — King of the Sandbox

Pitch: An adult who has proclaimed himself king of a sandcastle approximately 30 cm tall.

- Hook: A gigantic castle-shaped bucket worn upside down as a royal crown, with ridiculous battlements towering above his face.
- Silhouette at 40 m: Tall angular crown. A squat regular body beneath a disproportionately huge castle-shaped helmet.
- Face: 60% Happy / 40% Surprised. Cross-eyed royal grin, uneven buckteeth, tiny sandy goatee.
- Palette: Hot pink (dominant), sandy yellow, off-white; lime accent.
- Hand: Blue plastic bucket, dropped on danger. Worn: Giant castle crown secured to the head.
- Gag: He hides behind his little sandcastle, but his enormous crown remains fully visible. Players can spot him before he spots them.
- CIS reference: «Царь песочницы» — the self-appointed king of children's beach toys, except he is clearly an adult.
- Build: Low. One castle-shaped crown with an open face, head strap. Approximately 350–650 triangles.

### S3 — Knight of the Sandbox

Pitch: A delusional sandcastle warrior who treats a toy shovel like a legendary sword.

- Hook: One enormous purple plastic spade strapped diagonally across his back, extending high above his shoulder.
- Silhouette at 40 m: Long diagonal shovel shape, clearly separated from his small body. The shovel head is nearly as wide as his torso.
- Face: 25% Happy / 75% Surprised. Heroically raised eyebrows, two absurdly big front teeth.
- Palette: Purple (dominant), dusty beige, charcoal; yellow accent.
- Hand: Tiny castle mould, dropped on danger. Worn: Oversized back-mounted shovel and harness.
- Gag: He attempts to hide or run, but the enormous shovel sticks out behind him. Once ragdolled, he looks like a defeated knight with a completely useless plastic weapon.
- CIS reference: «Лопатка — меч-кладенец» — the sandbox shovel elevated to a legendary weapon.
- Build: Very low. Oversized plastic shovel, two straps, printed castle emblem. Approximately 250–500 triangles.

Animation limitation: All three can stand among finished sandcastles, holding their respective hand props, using the existing idle. If he must visibly scoop and build sandcastles during gameplay, that requires one new short scoop/build animation. None of the options can convincingly perform active construction using the current animation set alone.

## 3. Sunburnt lounger tourist

These concepts put the signature object on or in front of the body, never underneath the back. This is essential for visibility while lying on a 0.35 m lounger.

### B1 — SPF Body Armour

Pitch: A completely sunburnt idiot who bought sunscreen but wears the unopened bottle instead of applying it.

- Hook: An enormous factory-sealed SPF 100 sunscreen bottle strapped vertically across his chest like body armour. Its oversized mint-green cap sticks up beside his shoulder.
- Silhouette at 40 m: Long red-and-white slab. Thin body, 1.1 scale, with an elongated white bottle from upper chest to thighs. Thick white straps wrap around the back.
- Face: 65% Worried / 35% Surprised. Bright-red nose, large pale untanned circles around the eyes, clenched little teeth.
- Palette: Tomato red (dominant), ivory white, charcoal; mint-green accent.
- Hand: Tiny battery-powered fan, dropped on danger. Worn: Giant sunscreen-bottle armour and straps.
- Gag: On the lounger, the player sees a burned-red body with a massive unopened sunscreen bottle on top. On danger, he drops the fan and flees. When dragged into the van, the white bottle remains attached like a gigantic product label.
- CIS reference: «Первый день в Анапе — уже сгорел. Крем купил, но не намазался».
- Build: Low-medium. Oversized bottle shell, cap, label decal and flat back straps. Use two foam sections attached to torso/hips to preserve ragdoll bending. Approximately 450–850 triangles.

### B2 — The Human Solar Cooker

Pitch: A man who attempted to tan faster by turning himself into a solar oven.

- Hook: An enormous folded aluminium tanning reflector mounted across his chest, with two bright silver angular panels forming a V.
- Silhouette at 40 m: Sharp metallic butterfly shape across a red body. Both panels project to the sides of the torso.
- Face: 85% Worried / 15% Blink. Unburnt pale rings around his eyes, scarlet cheeks, tiny miserable mouth.
- Palette: Silver (dominant), burnt red, navy; fluorescent orange accent.
- Hand: Small aloe spray bottle, dropped on danger. Worn: Rigid foil reflector panels and chest frame.
- Gag: On the lounger he resembles a piece of food inside a solar cooker. When he flees, his silver reflectors remain ridiculously prominent. They also create an awkward angular shape when players drag him into the van.
- CIS reference: «Жарится как шашлык» — somebody determined to get a tan and instead getting roasted.
- Build: Very low. Two folded foil panels and a shallow chest frame. Approximately 200–450 triangles.

### B3 — The Grilled Tourist

Pitch: A sunburnt tourist who looks as though he has been prepared for the oven.

- Hook: A gigantic disposable aluminium roasting tray strapped across the front of his torso, framing his tomato-red body like a baked potato.
- Silhouette at 40 m: Large shallow silver oval/rectangle projecting from the torso. Clearly visible from above and from the side while reclining.
- Face: 70% Worried / 20% Surprised / 10% Happy. White goggle-shaped tan lines, crooked clenched teeth, bright-red nose.
- Palette: Tomato red, aluminium silver (dominant on the front), faded beige; turquoise accent.
- Hand: Tiny cooling fan, dropped on danger. Worn: Roasting tray attached to a front chest harness.
- Gag: The tray remains around him when he is dragged off the lounger and loaded into the van. From above, he still resembles an overcooked potato being carried away in its tray.
- CIS reference: «Турист — курица гриль» — someone who has completely overdone his sunbathing.
- Build: Low. One shallow bevelled roasting tray with rolled rim, harness. Approximately 250–500 triangles.

Lounger test: For B1, the sunscreen bottle and label face straight upward. For B2, both reflector panels are visible from above. For B3, the silver tray frames the body. All three use the existing lie-on-back pose with a placement offset for the lounger; no new animation is needed. B1 has the strongest combination of front, side and walking readability.

## 4. Dumb macho lifeguard

### L1 — Inflatable Bodybuilder

Pitch: A lifeguard who wants everybody to admire his muscles, except his muscles are inflatable.

- Hook: Two gigantic inflatable armbands sculpted and painted to resemble exaggerated bodybuilder biceps. Inflation valves are visible up close.
- Silhouette at 40 m: Massive inverted triangle. Extremely wide upper arms, narrow waist, maximum muscle slider. Idle arm angle 56° to keep the inflated biceps visibly separated from the torso.
- Face: 90% Happy / 10% Surprised. Stupidly confident grin, perfect square teeth, tiny pencil moustache, one raised eyebrow.
- Palette: Cobalt blue (dominant), white, tan; safety-orange accent.
- Hand: Orange rescue float, dropped on danger. Worn: Inflatable biceps, blue sleeveless vest marked «СПАСАТЕЛЬ».
- Gag: At the first sign of danger, he drops his rescue equipment and runs away. Even unconscious, his ridiculous inflated arms remain permanently heroic. Players have to manoeuvre his oversized shoulders into the van.
- CIS reference: The self-proclaimed «главный спасатель пляжа» who acts like a bodybuilder but is terrified of actual danger.
- Build: Low-medium. Two oversized inflatable bicep meshes with valves, sleeveless vest. Approximately 600–1,100 triangles.

### L2 — The Lifebuoy Halo

Pitch: A self-important beach hero who wears his rescue equipment like a saint's halo.

- Hook: A gigantic orange-and-white lifebuoy mounted vertically behind his shoulders, framing his head like an enormous halo.
- Silhouette at 40 m: Giant circular outline around the head and shoulders. The ring extends beyond both shoulders and above his hat.
- Face: 80% Happy / 20% Worried. Huge self-satisfied smile, painted-on sharp moustache, artificially perfect teeth.
- Palette: Safety orange (dominant), white, navy; cyan accent.
- Hand: Phone for heroic selfies, dropped on danger. Worn: Huge lifebuoy, upper-back harness, orange rescue vest.
- Gag: He hides behind beach furniture, but his enormous rescue halo gives him away. When unconscious, the ring continues to frame his stupidly smiling face inside the van.
- CIS reference: The beach rescuer whose favourite activity is posing, not rescuing. «За буйки не заплывать!»
- Build: Low. One thick low-poly lifebuoy, four white segments, harness. Approximately 350–650 triangles.

### L3 — The Hero with His Own Rescue Dummy

Pitch: A muscular lifeguard permanently carrying proof of a rescue that never happened.

- Hook: An orange foam rescue-training mannequin strapped upright onto his back, like a trophy. The mannequin wears a painted copy of his stupid moustache.
- Silhouette at 40 m: Two-headed silhouette. One real head and a second clearly artificial mannequin head protruding above the shoulder.
- Face: 75% Happy / 25% Surprised. Overconfident half-lidded eyes, wide grin, one prominent tooth.
- Palette: Deep blue (dominant), rescue orange, white; black accent.
- Hand: Small rescue baton, dropped on danger. Worn: Inert foam mannequin and heavy backpack straps.
- Gag: Dragging this lifeguard looks like dragging two people simultaneously. Loading him into the van creates an absurd tangle of real ragdoll limbs and rigid fake mannequin limbs.
- CIS reference: A show-off beach rescuer treating a rescue-training mannequin as evidence of his heroic achievements.
- Build: Medium. Simplified rigid adult-proportioned foam dummy, no extra skeleton or animation, plus straps. Approximately 1,000–2,000 triangles.

## Final selection — four characters that read as one set

C1 — Corn Minaret | Tall / Yellow

The towering corn rack makes his profession unmistakable from far away, even in a crowd.

S1 — Walking Fortress | Low / Lime green

A squat toy castle with adult legs is a complete visual joke, without needing a building animation.

B1 — SPF Body Armour | Long / Red and white

The unopened sunscreen bottle explains both his stupidity and his sunburn, whether standing or lying down.

L1 — Inflatable Bodybuilder | Wide / Cobalt blue

Fake muscles create a permanent heroic pose using the existing skeleton, making his cowardly panic funnier.

### One-glance silhouette hierarchy

Conceptual mass-and-silhouette test, not final model proportions. The distinctive forms are the corn tower, castle block, sunscreen slab and inflated shoulders.

### Production rules for the final four

- Keep accessories physically manageable. Tall racks and shoulder pads should use simplified collision shapes. They can visually scrape the van, but must not make loading impossible.
- Don't add bespoke animations. Use existing idle, panic, hide, lie, drag and ragdoll behaviour. Only commission a scoop animation if visible sandcastle construction is essential.
- Keep faces secondary at distance. At 40 m, identify each character from its silhouette and dominant colour, not teeth, labels or expression.
- Prioritise the four giant hooks. Everything else—shoes, shorts, moustaches, little tools—supports the character without competing for attention.

The important difference from the rejected v01 beach cast: these aren't four tourists wearing slightly different outfits. They're four visually distinct idiots, each with a recognisable physical joke that survives the transition from standing NPC to floppy ragdoll.
