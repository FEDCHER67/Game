You are a character designer helping the lead of a small indie game. Answer in English, compact markdown, no preamble.

## The game
VOLUNTEERS ONLY: a first-person co-op (1-4 players) physical-comedy game. Players knock out cartoon ADULT NPCs, physically drag the floppy ragdoll into their van, drive to a hideout and do a short cartoon "operation" (no gore). Systemic comedy: stories come from players, NPCs, props and the van. The world mixes post-Soviet / CIS everyday life (courtyards, village, dachas, beach resorts like Anapa or Sochi) with a stylised American downtown. Humour is meme-literate and absurd, never sexual, never gory.

## Art and technical limits
- Stylised low-poly "sausage" NPCs: one shared body (a tall capsule head-and-neck, big round eyes, round nose, mitten hands), one shared skeleton and one shared animation set (idle, panic run, trip, play dead, hide, phone the police, double-take flee...).
- A type = clothes, hair, props, face paint, facial hair/teeth/glasses, a baked face (a mix of Happy, Surprised, Worried, Blink), body sliders (belly, thin, muscle, bust, glutes, game scale 0.9-1.1), the hang angle of the arms in idle (normally 64-72 degrees down; a small value means arms held out from the body), skin colour.
- Hand props are rigid in the hands and drop to the floor at the first danger. Budget about 12-18k triangles. A type must read in one second at 30-50 m by silhouette and colour.

## What I need
1. A blunt diagnosis, max 8 bullets: why the five in Image 1 read as generic slop next to Image 2. Be concrete (silhouette, proportions, colour, props, faces, cultural specificity).
2. For EACH of the five roles: 3 concept options. Each option is built around ONE strong signature hook and lists:
   - pitch (one line);

   - silhouette change readable at 40 m that only this type has (hat, big prop, body slider, arm angle...);
   - face (expression mix plus paint, facial hair, teeth, glasses);
   - palette: 2-3 colours plus one accent;
   - in-game gag that uses our mechanics (noticing the player, fleeing, being grabbed and dragged, being loaded into the van, the operation, dropping hand props, being a witness); no new animations unless unavoidable, and say so if one is needed;
   - the CIS or meme reference Vadim would recognise;
   - build cost (what new geometry).
3. Your single best option per role, with a one-line reason.
4. Two extra beach types that would be funnier than any of these five.

## Rules
- Adults only. No sexualised content. No likeness of a specific real person (parody archetypes are fine; flag any idea that leans on a real person). No gore.
- The nudist stays Steam-safe (keep the pixel censor or use a comic cover).
- Plain socks with sandals, plain sunglasses, a plain panama or a plain towel are NOT hooks on their own. Avoid generic "beach tourist" filler.
