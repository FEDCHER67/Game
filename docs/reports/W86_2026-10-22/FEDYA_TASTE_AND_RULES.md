# Fedya's taste — how to pick 1 of 3 variants on his behalf (2026-10-22)

Fedya's order (22.10): every material on the whole map (Forest, Hamlet, Rough, Downtown, Construction, Port, Elite, Quiet Valley, Beach, Tramduro, world: roads, curbs, bridges, tunnel, fences, rocks, water edges, props) gets textures found on the internet (CC0 / free licence only, sources recorded) and repainted to our style; for every group make THREE variants, pick ONE yourself using the taste below, roll it out; keep the other two ready (materials + a sheet) so Fedya can switch if he dislikes the pick. Groups with verdict НЕТ / ПЛОХО / ОК-НО-НЕ-СОГЛАСОВАНО in `Working/Reviews/MapW86_TextureInventory_2026-10-22/INVENTORY.md` all get this. Already chosen but not rolled out → just roll out (O1, DS1 80 % / DS2+DS3 20 % random, DC3, port P1 containers + P3 fence/vehicles/buildings, E2, C2, Q3 — `Working/Reviews/MapW86_Textures_2026-10-22/DECISIONS_FEDYA.md`). Placeholder boxes (~165 props) get real low-poly models, not textures.

## What Fedya picked before (use as the compass)
- Overall: One-Armed Robber look — clean painted colours, soft volume, readable shapes, NO photo grime, no acid colours. Real 3D depth beats painted-on detail every time (he rejected flat facades with painted windows).
- Hamlet house H5: log cabin (сруб), brick plinth, steep DARK profiled-metal roof, carved window trims (наличники) + shutters, porch, three trim colour schemes mixed. Weathered but clean. No street lamps in the Hamlet.
- Rough R2c: Soviet panel block KH2, real 3D balconies, entrances with canopies, downpipes, glass of varying dirtiness; grey, foggy, dull district; dry yellow-brown grass.
- Downtown D4: composition D1, smooth plaster + brick, restrained but wider palette (dark blue-grey, brick red, olive, ochre), recessed windows, cornices, awnings rare, fire escapes in yards. Offices O1 = travertine stone grid with bronze frames (classic, solid, not glass). Separation DS1 = pilasters + cornice breaks. Square DC3 = cool grey stone.
- Towers T4: six-sided twisted twins, expensive glass to the ground.
- Port: containers multicolour (P1), fence + yard machines + buildings P3. Elite E2. Construction C2. Quiet Valley Q3.
- Light: lamps dimmer, functional flicker in the city; fog only +8 %; roofs darker than first proposals.
- Text on the map: no Russian signage where it reads as a brand; the valley "casino" sign has no name; no logos/brands anywhere.
- Performance matters (FPS was a big topic): shared materials, atlases, vertex colours, 1024 px textures (2048 only for big atlases), LODs on models.

## Picking rule
Pick the variant that (1) has real depth/shape over painted detail, (2) fits the district mood (Hamlet warmest/brightest rustic, Rough grey Soviet, Downtown American restrained, Construction bright, Port industrial colourful containers, Elite expensive calm Mediterranean, Valley mysterious calm green, Beach white-sand Miami, Tramduro palace + military), (3) matches neighbours already approved, (4) costs fewer FPS.
