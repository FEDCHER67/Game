## 0. Коротко для Феди

Телефон — общий центр дел команды с личным управлением у каждого игрока. Сохраняем существующие серебристый корпус, обои с бусиком, значки, шрифты, пропорции и домашнюю сетку; аппарат располагается в правой трети вида без навязанного нового размера. WASD позволяет ходить, мышь управляет телефоном, удержание ПКМ позволяет осматриваться. Водитель открывает телефон с ручником при скорости не более 0,1 м/с; при потере этих условий аппарат убирается, а уже начатый разговор продолжается звуком. Передача звонка требует явного согласия получателя и не обходит ограничения водителя.

Переписки, встречи, заказы, чеки, контакты и фонд общие; прочтение и результаты сканирования личные. Сканер доступен из принятого спецзаказа: 2,5 реальные секунды непрерывного наведения, до 10 м, прямая видимость открытого лица живого NPC. Карта находится в телефоне, показывает товарищей и поддерживает личную точку с временной командной отметкой. С убранным телефоном выбранная точка обозначается значком в мире и расстоянием.

Все ответы 1–69 внесены. Обычный баннер показывается 7 реальных секунд; командная отметка живёт 30 секунд, одна на игрока, отправка не чаще раза в секунду. Карта плоская, север сверху; результат сканирования виден 2 секунды, повтор по тому же NPC — после отвода и нового наведения. Храним последние 200 записей каждой переписки и общей ленты, личное прочтение и расстановку значков сохраняем. Открытые параметры по ответу 30Б закрыты отдельным интервью; в Questions left open отмечены только оставшиеся зависимости ресурсов и интеграции. Все цены остаются заглушками.

### 2.2 Inherited contracts — Confirmed

- A buyer's incoming call reaches all connected participants. The first successful answer owns it; other phones show who is talking. Different players may speak to different buyers concurrently; one player conducts at most one call.
- Incoming calls last 15 real seconds. A first missed/declined team call produces one retry after one game hour; after the second unanswered call the buyer leaves a message. One player's local dismissal is not a team miss while someone else can still answer.
- Normal voice is private to the participant. Speakerphone is audible to nearby players up to 10 m, with attenuation, and does not broadcast across the map.
- Conversations, contacts, business terms, appointments, orders, funds and respect are shared. Scan findings are private; a scan does not mark the target found for the team.
- The shared world remains active while using the phone. Instruments and carrying require putting the phone away; phone UI does not supply virtual tools or remote physical merchandise handover.
- One active special order exists per team. Requirements, bargaining, acceptance, expiry, delivery and rewards follow S3/S4, with their later Decisions taking precedence.
- The foreman has outings in Forest, Khutor and Rough only; Stroyka is his untimed office, not an outing destination. First sales slice: Forest outing plus the office.
- S9 K2 grants the immediately arriving foreman/surgeon a free cancellation window until first registered team attendance or one game hour after booking, whichever happens first. C01 retains the pre-arrival free window. Attendance and subsequent completion use the existing sales rules.
- Booking does not assign the negotiator for an in-person sale. The player who starts local bargaining receives that role, as resolved by S9 K1. Phone order bargaining has the separate PH-D10 owner.
- Negotiation ranks are per player, purchased using shared free team XP; anesthesia unlocks remain shared. Personal earned XP is contribution statistics, not a separate spendable wallet.
- FishNet server/host owns shared business state and the save. Phone rendering/input are local. Solo is a host with no clients; host departure ends the session, without migration.
- Closing the host freezes active-world time. Client departure does not freeze the world. S5's appended Decision 5 retains the departing player's entire inventory, including a held cooler and contents, in the host's per-player save; it does not drop that inventory for the team.

## 5. Vehicle access and driver-safe handoff

Passengers can use the full phone. Existing passenger movement/riding-slot rules still apply; opening the phone does not move a passenger into a different seat.

For a driver, full phone access and any new call acceptance require both conditions: vehicle speed magnitude is at or below 0.1 m/s (approximately 0.36 km/h), and its handbrake is engaged. PH-D61 supplies this stop tolerance for small physical jitter; reverse speed is not a negative-speed loophole. Stopping in place without the handbrake is insufficient. Taking a handoff never bypasses this gate.

When speed exceeds that tolerance, or the handbrake condition ceases to hold, the full phone is put away. A call already owned by the driver continues as audio. While the gate is false the driver cannot select replies, accept a new call/handoff, bargain, buy, cancel or toggle phone controls. After stopping within the tolerance with the handbrake, the player can reopen the phone and regain controls. No automatic reply or automatic fullscreen reopening occurs.

An audio-only call is still that player's one call. It does not let a moving driver take a second buyer. Audible continuation does not automatically enable speakerphone.

Handoff sequence:

1. The current caller selects a connected teammate. Distance across the map does not prevent a transfer request.
2. The request is a non-modal notice; it does not force open a phone or switch cameras.
3. The recipient explicitly accepts while eligible: no other active call, no blocking work/carry mode, and, if driving, stopped with the handbrake.
4. Eligibility is checked again at the authoritative transfer. If driving resumed or another call was acquired, no transfer occurs and the sender retains the call.
5. Only successful acceptance transfers ownership. Dialogue position and commercial terms are preserved; no new introduction, free bargain attempt or restarted incoming-call window is created.

A handoff request lasts 15 real seconds from issue. A moving driver may see the pending non-modal request but has no actionable acceptance until eligible, and only within the same remaining request window. Decline or expiry leaves the call with its sender. The sender also retains ownership throughout the wait. Successful acceptance switches speakerphone off for the new caller under PH-D49. A request never restarts a buyer's original ring, dialogue or commercial deadline. If the underlying call ends, invalidate its pending transfer; a full host restart ends answered calls under PH-D50 and therefore clears their transfer requests.

## 6. Calls and shared conversations

### 6.1 Incoming calls and local dismissal

All session participants receive the same incoming-call identity and the same 15-real-second deadline. Opening the phone does not answer automatically. Show the buyer and explicit Answer/Decline choices. The first valid answer wins; the server resolves simultaneous answers once, stops ringing on other phones and publishes Talking: player name.

Decline dismisses the call only locally. Another participant may answer during the remainder of the original window. Local dismissal does not restart that window, count as a commercial refusal or incur respect loss. If nobody answers, apply the existing team-level retry/message sequence once, not once per player.

Different buyers can have concurrent calls with different participants. A player already talking gets the second buyer's banner but cannot answer until ending the first conversation; the second call's original deadline continues. Other participants remain free to answer it. There is no new held-call queue or second active conversation on that player.

### 6.2 Conversation ownership and interruption

The current participant owns the dialogue choices for the ordinary call. Shared written terms remain visible to the team. Voluntary transfer follows §5, including eligibility for non-drivers. ### 12.4 Full host restart and old saves — Confirmed recovery policy

| Saved situation | Required restored result |
|---|---|
| Phone was open on any screen | Load with it hidden; next ordinary opening is Home, using the existing design and that player's saved icon arrangement. Do not restore an actionable confirmation dialog. |
| Ordinary call had already been answered | End voice and retain/post the same conditions and available replies in the common thread, without restarting any offer deadline. Clear transfer requests belonging to that ended call. |
| Incoming call had not been answered | Continue only the saved remainder of its 15-second answer window and its same retry/message phase. Do not count host closure as another miss. |
| Buyer intro had begun | Treat it as shown; restore normal view without replaying the remainder or making a second buyer. Its conditions remain in the conversation. |
| Post-sale Continue/Finish question was active | Restore the saved remainder of 30 real seconds; no time was consumed offline. Already completed payment/rewards remain unchanged. |
| A scan/result or ordinary banner was being shown | Return to normal hidden-phone presentation. Required private findings and retained history remain data, not a fresh automatically replayed scan/banner or reward. |
| Temporary team pings existed | Clear them. Saved personal destinations remain. |
| Histories/read state existed | Restore retained 200-entry histories and personal read/unread state. History restoration does not generate fresh transaction events. |

For an older save without phone history, perform one migration initialization in the existing save: preserve actual money, progress and known active commitments; add one system entry explaining the transfer; populate current commitment cards from real data. Record that initialization so another load does not add the entry again. Do not invent old receipts, historical sale times, missing private findings or retroactive reward/penalty events. Technical DTO/version details remain implementation work under PH-P02, not another unasked gameplay preset.
