## 0. Коротко для Феди

Телефон — общий центр дел команды с личным управлением у каждого игрока. Он занимает правую треть экрана: WASD позволяет ходить, мышь управляет телефоном, удержание ПКМ позволяет осматриваться. Водитель открывает телефон только после остановки с ручником; при начале движения аппарат убирается, а уже начатый разговор продолжается звуком. Передача звонка водителю требует тех же условий и его явного согласия.

Переписки, встречи, заказы, чеки, контакты и фонд общие; прочтение и результаты сканирования личные. Сканер доступен из принятого спецзаказа: 2,5 реальные секунды непрерывного наведения, до 10 м, прямая видимость открытого лица живого NPC. Карта находится в телефоне, показывает товарищей и поддерживает личную точку с временной командной отметкой. С убранным телефоном выбранная точка обозначается значком в мире и расстоянием.

Ответы 1–29 зафиксированы ниже. В ответе 30 выбран вариант Б: оставшиеся мелкие параметры нужно обсудить отдельно. По последующей просьбе Феди документ записан сейчас; эти пункты перечислены в Questions left open и не объявлены утверждёнными пресетами. Все цены остаются заглушками.

### 2.2 Inherited contracts — Confirmed

- A buyer's incoming call reaches all connected participants. The first successful answer owns it; other phones show who is talking. Different players may speak to different buyers concurrently; one player conducts at most one call.
- Incoming calls last 15 real seconds. A first missed/declined team call produces one retry after one game hour; after the second unanswered call the buyer leaves a message. One player's local dismissal is not a team miss while someone else can still answer.
- Normal voice is private to the participant. Speakerphone is audible to nearby players up to 10 m, with attenuation, and does not broadcast across the map.
- Conversations, contacts, business terms, appointments, orders, funds and respect are shared. Scan findings are private; a scan does not mark the target found for the team.
- The shared world remains active while using the phone. Instruments and carrying require putting the phone away; phone UI does not supply virtual tools or remote physical merchandise handover.
- One active special order exists per team. Requirements, bargaining, acceptance, expiry, delivery and rewards follow S3/S4, with their later Decisions taking precedence.
- The foreman has outings in Forest, Khutor and Rough only; Stroyka is his untimed office, not an outing destination. First sales slice: Forest outing plus the office.
- S9 K2 grants the immediately arriving foreman/surgeon a free cancellation window until first registered team attendance or one game hour after booking, whichever happens first. C01 retains the pre-arrival free window. Attendance and subsequent completion use the existing sales rules.
- Booking does not assign the negotiator for an in-person sale. The player who starts local bargaining receives that role, as resolved by S9 K1. Phone order bargaining has the separate PH-D10 owner.
- Negotiation ranks are per player, purchased using shared free team XP; anesthesia unlocks remain shared. Personal earned XP is contribution statistics, not a separate spendable wallet.
- FishNet server/host owns shared business state and the save. Phone rendering/input are local. Solo is a host with no clients; host departure ends the session, without migration.
- Closing the host freezes active-world time. Client departure does not freeze the world. S5's appended Decision 5 retains the departing player's entire inventory, including a held cooler and contents, in the host's per-player save; it does not drop that inventory for the team.

## 4. Physical presentation and input arbitration

### 4.1 On foot

The phone is in the player's hands, with its readable screen occupying approximately the right third of the view. The remainder shows the active world. The exact safe area, text size and panel dimensions are open; the chosen proportion is confirmed.

| Input / state | Required result |
|---|---|
| Tab in ordinary on-foot play | Open/close the phone. Ordinary opening lands on Home. |
| WASD with phone out | Walk using existing movement; do not apply the old phone MovementLocked behavior. |
| Mouse with phone out | Move the phone cursor. |
| Hold RMB | Look around; releasing returns mouse control to the phone. Phone buttons must not click through during look input. |
| LMB over phone UI | Operate the phone, not a tool, body grab or world object. |
| Phone out | Hotbar actions are inactive and the selected item is remembered. A small held item is lowered rather than dropped or duplicated. |
| Tool use / body dragging / two-handed carry | Put the phone away before the action. A held body or load must be released/set down manually before opening the phone. |
| Table mode | Tab retains its existing Prep/Operation meaning; it must not also open the phone. |

No incoming notification automatically releases a grip, puts down a cooler, uses a syringe or changes a table interaction. A banner is permitted while the full phone is unavailable. Answering remains subject to eligibility and the original call deadline.

S9 K5 is resolved as a target behavior change: the existing movement lock and whole-van prohibition are prototype gaps. Walking/look access is already confirmed by S4 SG-D55; this interview supplies controls and the driver/passenger rules. No Unity change is made by authoring this document.

### 4.2 Modes that block answering

In operation/table mode or an in-person negotiation, incoming calls show the banner only. The player explicitly leaves the current mode before answering or accepting a call handoff. Pressing an unavailable answer control must not automatically exit the mode. World timers, operation progress and used bargaining attempts continue under their existing rules.

The phone does not override buyer intro camera policy. The later S4 Decision 4 protects a driver with a medium-size, clearly noticeable picture-in-picture intro, without full-screen camera takeover or fade. Other participants follow that updated sales intro policy. The five-second intro and skip behavior remain sales contracts; exact overlap with a phone banner is open.

## 5. Vehicle access and driver-safe handoff

Passengers can use the full phone. Existing passenger movement/riding-slot rules still apply; opening the phone does not move a passenger into a different seat.

For a driver, full phone access and any new call acceptance require both conditions: the vehicle is stopped and its handbrake is engaged. Stopping in place without the handbrake is insufficient. Taking a handoff never bypasses this gate.

When the vehicle begins moving, or the handbrake condition ceases to hold, the full phone is put away. A call already owned by the driver continues as audio. While the gate is false the driver cannot select replies, accept a new call/handoff, bargain, buy, cancel or toggle phone controls. After stopping with the handbrake, the player can reopen the phone and regain controls. No automatic reply or automatic fullscreen reopening occurs.

An audio-only call is still that player's one call. It does not let a moving driver take a second buyer. Audible continuation does not automatically enable speakerphone.

Handoff sequence:

1. The current caller selects a connected teammate. Distance across the map does not prevent a transfer request.
2. The request is a non-modal notice; it does not force open a phone or switch cameras.
3. The recipient explicitly accepts while eligible: no other active call, no blocking work/carry mode, and, if driving, stopped with the handbrake.
4. Eligibility is checked again at the authoritative transfer. If driving resumed or another call was acquired, no transfer occurs and the sender retains the call.
5. Only successful acceptance transfers ownership. Dialogue position and commercial terms are preserved; no new introduction, free bargain attempt or restarted incoming-call window is created.

A moving driver's request can remain visible as pending according to the unresolved request-lifetime policy, but has no actionable acceptance control until eligibility returns. Decline, an unavailable recipient or an expired request must never leave a call with two owners. The sender remains owner while a request awaits acceptance. Speakerphone transition on handoff and the request timeout are open details, not silently chosen presets.

## 6. Calls and shared conversations

### 6.1 Incoming calls and local dismissal

All session participants receive the same incoming-call identity and the same 15-real-second deadline. Opening the phone does not answer automatically. Show the buyer and explicit Answer/Decline choices. The first valid answer wins; the server resolves simultaneous answers once, stops ringing on other phones and publishes Talking: player name.

Decline dismisses the call only locally. Another participant may answer during the remainder of the original window. Local dismissal does not restart that window, count as a commercial refusal or incur respect loss. If nobody answers, apply the existing team-level retry/message sequence once, not once per player.

Different buyers can have concurrent calls with different participants. A player already talking gets the second buyer's banner but cannot answer until ending the first conversation; the second call's original deadline continues. Other participants remain free to answer it. There is no new held-call queue or second active conversation on that player.

### 6.2 Conversation ownership and interruption

The current participant owns the dialogue choices for the ordinary call. Shared written terms remain visible to the team. Voluntary transfer follows §5, including eligibility for non-drivers. If that ordinary caller disconnects, end the call and place its conditions and available replies in the shared conversation; do not automatically assign the voice call to a moving driver or ring everybody again.

If the same participant also owned special-order bargaining, preserve those commercial terms/attempts and release only their claim so another participant can take over under §7.1. A disconnect is not a cancellation or a new order acceptance.

Speakerphone begins off for each new call. Its enabled state is visible. Private voice is heard only by the caller; enabled speakerphone places the same buyer line at the current caller's phone/player position, with attenuation and no sound beyond 10 m. Do not send the voice to the whole team just because the thread is shared.

The foreman keeps his comic gibberish voice, without spoken-gibberish subtitles; written conditions and reply buttons carry intelligible terms. C01 and the surgeon retain their own recognizable voices with the phone effect. Existing voice direction is inherited from S4, not replaced with a new text-to-speech requirement.

### 6.3 Threads, offers and response timers

Contacts lead to the buyer's common thread. Any participant can inspect history, terms, current deal and receipt without acquiring negotiation authority. Read status stays personal. Reading never counts as responding.

For an offer requiring a response, the server owns one response cycle: 12 game hours of silence, then one −1 respect consequence and 12 game hours of unavailability, then a fresh response window. Valid Discuss or Not interested from an eligible participant ends waiting for that cycle; opening a message or dismissing a call does not. Discuss starts negotiation, not a special order's 48-hour deadline.

Informational messages, receipts, arrival notices and reminders for an already accepted commitment do not start the silence-penalty cycle. Templates must distinguish information from response-required offers; the exact template list is open.

The later bodybuilder refusal rule overrides older generic refusal wording: declining that unaccepted order costs −1 respect and it returns after one game day. Cancelling an already accepted order is a different action with the S3/S4 consequences in §8. Show an extra warning before the penalized refusal under PH-D11.

## 7. Bargaining, appointments and receipts

### 7.1 Special-order discussion ownership

The first valid Discuss action claims the offer's bargaining role and ends its unanswered-offer cycle. Other participants see the current negotiator and read the current terms without actionable bargaining controls. A stale Discuss or Not interested from another phone must not overwrite an already claimed or accepted state.

The owner gets the existing +5/+10/+20% choices, full short reply text, current amount and exact chance using their personal negotiation rank and the shared buyer's respect tier. The source cap is 95%. First refusal counteroffers remain +20 → +10, +10 → +5, +5 → base price. Only permitted smaller requests remain for the second attempt.

A second refusal applies the existing −1 respect consequence and three-game-hour offer lockout. No special order has been accepted and its 48-hour timer has not started. Handoff or takeover preserves attempts, agreed percentage and lockout. Remaining attempts use the new negotiator's rank; closed/reopened screens cannot reroll used attempts.

An eligible teammate can receive an explicit voluntary handoff; a disconnected owner can be replaced. As with calls, the driver gate and blocking work modes apply to interactive phone bargaining. Agreeing on terms is followed by explicit acceptance of the order, with its requirements and deadline displayed. Reading, Discuss, opening the scanner link or looking at a photo is not acceptance.

### 7.2 Booking flow

From a contact/thread, the player selects the meeting action and, for C01, an owned base. The preview contains buyer, destination, arrival time, end of waiting and cancellation terms. Book meeting is a separate explicit action. If availability or terms have changed before confirmation, show the updated preview rather than silently booking another location or a duplicate meeting.

The shared appointment card and map marker appear after authoritative success. Show the booking participant for clarity, but do not give that participant automatic ownership of the later in-person trade. One appointment per buyer may coexist with appointments for other buyers.

| Buyer | Booking and phone status |
|---|---|
| C01 | Available 24/7; choose an owned base; arrives after one game hour and waits three more. The selected base's anchor remains fixed for that appointment. |
| Foreman | Available 24/7; arrives immediately and waits four game hours. Outing rotation uses Forest, Khutor and Rough, with no repeats until available points are exhausted. Stroyka is excluded. |
| Foreman's office | After the normal unlock, trade directly at his Stroyka desk without booking or an appointment deadline. A phone outing may transfer the single buyer away from the office under S4, preserving commercial state. |
| Surgeon | Book only in 18:00–06:00; immediate arrival; waits until the earlier of four game hours after booking or the next 06:00. Two Downtown points and one Stroyka point use their own rotation. |

The card displays arrival/waiting/ended status, absolute game deadline, remaining game time and relevant action availability. A new trade group cannot start after waiting ends; a group begun in time can finish under S3/S4. Phone use never pauses waiting, spoilage or buyer availability.

The foreman's office-to-outing transfer is one buyer and one continuing commercial context. Goods remain physically at the office; booking or map selection cannot teleport them. Conditions and attempts remain, and delivery/actual value must be checked again at the outing.

### 7.3 Cancel and refuse

Keep separate controls for Dismiss call, Decline unaccepted offer, Cancel appointment and Cancel accepted order. Only the relevant explicit commercial action affects its commitment. Phone closing and personal read state do not cancel an appointment.

| Action/state | Existing consequence to display |
|---|---|
| C01 appointment cancelled before arrival | Free. |
| Immediately arriving foreman/surgeon appointment, within K2's free window | Free until first registered team attendance or one game hour, whichever is earlier. |
| Penalized appointment cancellation / no-show | −1 respect once for that meeting, using the existing attendance/completion rules; not an extra charge for each phone. |
| Decline unaccepted bodybuilder order | −1 respect; returns after one game day. |
| Cancel accepted order before deadline | −2 respect; frees the shared slot. |
| Cancel accepted order after expiry | No additional penalty; frees the shared slot. |

Before a currently penalized cancellation/refusal, show its exact consequence and require the additional confirmation chosen in PH-D11. Free cancellation does not acquire this extra warning. When a free-window or deadline boundary changes during an open screen, re-evaluate before committing; never apply an undisclosed new penalty. Two phones cancelling the same commitment produce one result and at most its one applicable consequence.

The later buyer-violence rule also remains visible when relevant: hitting a buyer cancels the deal, costs −3 respect, leaves goods with the team and makes that buyer unavailable until the next game day. This phone document does not add a way to grab, carry or harvest the buyer.

### 7.4 Live deal and receipts

The common thread's live deal card is read-only for teammates who are not the in-person negotiator. It shows buyer, organ kind, quantity, quality grades, current quote, named respect tier, owner and remaining meeting status. An estimate in the phone is not remote sale authority.

Physical sale confirmation remains with the correct local negotiator; special-order handover remains a physical stash interaction. Actual quality, packaging, provenance, cooling history and saturation are evaluated by the existing sales/inventory contracts. A stale quoted amount causes a revised quote and another explicit confirmation; no item or funds change on the rejected old quote.

Successful transactions produce compact shared receipt cards. Expanding a receipt reveals:

- Buyer, transaction identity, game time and sale/order context.
- Sold kinds/counts and quality grades; enough item identity to distinguish one committed transaction from another.
- Placeholder base amounts and the applied quality, buyer, saturation, bargaining, order-bonus and delivery modifiers as applicable; final credited amount.
- Respect change with its reason, including a stated zero when a displayed event has no respect effect.
- XP awarded to each participant present at confirmation, separate novelty/on-time bonuses, and the total team XP increase.
- The committed result; reopening the receipt never reapplies payment, rewards or first-sale flags.

Regular organ sales use S4's base × quality × buyer coefficient × saturation × agreed bargain × delivery rule. Special orders use base × quality × order bonus × agreed bargain × delivery, without saturation or the surgeon's ordinary-sale coefficient. Monetary rounding remains the source's Proposed detail rather than a newly confirmed G41 choice. Blood uses its existing separate rules without organ quality or saturation.

Every participant in the session at sale confirmation gets the existing XP, irrespective of distance: 20 per organ or 5 per blood unit, plus applicable first-sale bonuses and +150 for on-time special completion. The catalogue's shared first-sale flags grant each bonus once per kind in that world. UI opening, reconnecting and notification replay are not reward events.

### 8.3 Scan eligibility and timing

Scanner entry is available only from the currently accepted active order. The existing accepted overdue order remains active until delivered or cancelled; expiry alone does not free its slot. Completed, cancelled and merely offered orders cannot start a new identification.

The scan considers one candidate NPC at a time. Eligibility requires a living NPC, visible face, direct line of sight and distance no greater than 10 m. Alive but asleep, downed, restrained, inside a van or on a table does not by itself disqualify the candidate if the face is actually visible. A head bag, a wall or a corpse prevents scanning. There is no identification through an opaque bag or from an exposed hand.

Maintain qualifying aim on the same candidate for **2.5 real seconds**. Changing candidates, losing framing/line of sight, exceeding range, hiding the face, the candidate dying or leaving scanner mode resets progress. Progress is never pooled across candidates or across interruptions. The scan must not spend game hours or borrow the world-clock rate for its duration.

At completion show a private result, logically Match / Not the target, with localized RU text such as Он / Не он. No eligible candidate is a targeting/visibility condition, not a false negative identity result. Exact reticle, progress animation and result dwell are open.

Scanner aiming must coexist with PH-D01's RMB look control; it cannot consume LMB as a tool action or change movement ownership. The exact reticle anchoring and scan-screen layout require the remaining UI discussion.

### 8.4 Identity, privacy and the twin rule

Match the order's authored eligible identity, not merely an NPC type, outfit, image similarity or the string HAMLET_BODYBUILDER on any generic crowd member. Follow the existing unique-person identity integration: a stable PersonId before capture, carried into the captured PatientId facts and organ provenance, with the unique authored role used for the approved twin replacement rule. NetworkObject IDs are not permanent identities.

If the special bodybuilder is dead before his order, the next morning's approved twin uses the same appearance/photo and becomes the order's target. A qualifying kidney from the original real bodybuilder already in the cooler also counts. These accepted donor identities must be represented by the existing provenance/role contract, not by accepting every NPC of the same type. A kidney obtained before acceptance can fulfill the order if it meets all requirements.

S1 §11's later decision remains in force: liver or second-kidney extraction is always fatal; first-kidney risk remains the specified chance with prior-blood modifiers. The phone neither rewrites those outcomes nor revives an NPC to allow a scan. A dead body cannot be scanned, while an already valid organ can still be checked for the order independently of scanning.

The scan result belongs only to the scanning player and is saved privately under that player's stable ID, as required by S4/S9 K11. Do not append it to the shared thread, notification history, teammate map or a team target-found flag. No overhead NPC marker, contour or continuing tracker is unlocked. Telling a teammate is the player's action.

An intentionally sent manual location ping remains possible, but is a static coordinate, never a target-following attachment or automatic disclosure of the scan result. A private past scan record must not become live positional knowledge after rejoining or after the twin replaces the previous target.

### 8.5 Delivery, deadlines and failures

Phone requirements and stash previews explain donor, kind, grade and packaging before confirmation. The team physically brings the merchandise to the unlocked stash and separately confirms there. Until success it remains retrievable. An unsuitable confirmed order attempt leaves the item with the team and applies the existing −1 respect consequence once per item per order; reading requirements or a preliminary check is free.

Ordinary buyer load rejection, including below-minimum goods under S9 K10, is not that special-order penalty. Do not display a misleading generic −1 for every failed UI or stale network request.

On-time delivery gives the inherited order reward. Expiry applies −4 respect once, removes only the +25% order bonus and preserves the agreed bargain percentage. The overdue order remains deliverable and occupies the slot. Cancelling after expiry adds no penalty; cancelling before expiry costs −2. Late UI reminders must not repeatedly apply the expiry consequence.

### 12.2 Concurrent actions

Use authoritative ownership and the existing atomic/idempotent transaction contract. PH-P02 describes a proposed data representation, not permission to weaken these required results:

- Two simultaneous answers produce one caller. Two Discuss actions produce one offer negotiator. Losing phones display the actual owner rather than keeping actionable stale controls.
- A moving driver cannot acquire a call through a racing handoff or stale stopped-state screen. A participant already in a call cannot acquire another through another phone flow.
- Accepting one order prevents a concurrent second acceptance into the team slot. The losing action spends nothing and starts no extra 48-hour timer.
- Booking/cancelling the same appointment twice produces one authoritative transition and one applicable consequence. A failed duplicate is not a new no-show or refusal.
- An item is sold once. Item transfer, shared money, XP, respect, saturation, first-sale flags and receipt identity are coherent with the existing sale transaction.
- Purchase races cannot spend the same shared funds twice. Reopening a receipt or replaying a notification never resends a commercial action.
- A stale quote/preview returns the changed state for review. Do not silently use a new price, destination, cancellation penalty or owner behind an old confirmation button.

### 12.3 Join, leave and clocks

Late joiners receive current contacts, active meetings/orders, fund, offer/negotiator state and the applicable shared read model. They do not receive another player's private scans or automatic replay of a buyer call as a fresh 15-second window. Receipt visibility is not retroactive XP entitlement: the participants present at the original transaction remain its recorded recipients.

On client departure, other clients and the host keep running. An ordinary caller departure follows PH-D26; order bargaining can be taken over without new attempts. The inventory retention rule is independent of that commercial takeover. A pending handoff must not transfer to a departed player.

Only the host writes the world/business save, including the existing per-player blocks keyed by the persistent player ID. Clients do not write an independent business save. Existing local camera photos are outside this specification's business-save scope.

When the host leaves, the session ends for everyone. Save on the existing host lifecycle and use its existing autosave policy; do not introduce host migration. Game-world deadlines resume from the saved active-world state and do not advance while the host world is closed. A client leaving or a phone being opened does not stop those deadlines.

Keep clock domains separate: incoming ring = 15 real seconds; scan = 2.5 real seconds; source buyer intro = 5 real seconds; post-sale Continue/Finish choice = 30 real seconds. Appointment, silence-cycle, order, power and reoffer deadlines use authoritative game time. No phone app creates its own game-day rate. The existing M8 world-clock integration must settle the known build/spec day-length mismatch; this phone spec does not select a new rate. Recovery of short real-time UI sequences across a full host restart remains an explicit open question.
