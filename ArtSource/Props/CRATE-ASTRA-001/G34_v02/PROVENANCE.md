# CRATE / G34 collision revision v02

Created locally in Blender 5.2.1 LTS, background CLI only.

Pine shipping crate with visible slats, graphite corner brackets, baked up-arrow/fragile-glass stencils, and three steel drone lifting eyelets; lid open and closed. All geometry and colour atlases were authored locally; existing Astra colours/chamfers were used as the style baseline.

No downloaded assets, paid generation, live Blender control, Unity import, commits or pushes. UCX meshes are local convex prototypes for the future Unity import postprocessor. Visual approval remains with Fedya.
