## Recommended A — chunky cash bundle

- Main shape: one broad, thick banknote bundle with slightly uneven layered edges and a wide central paper band. Use the simple pickup readability of [Sketchfab / Dekrayzis cash bundle](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/cash-bundle-19c1558f1bbb403b943f338a9a70da89) as a silhouette reference; create new geometry and fictional print.
- Stylization: soft bevels, few large paper-layer steps, no individual high-detail bill geometry. A single bundle should read as money at arm's length; two or three instances can form a pile later without another Tripo generation.
- Color direction after Tripo: muted green or teal-gray notes, warm cream paper edges, a contrasting pale band, a simple fictional emblem. Keep matte like the game's finished organs but use a more graphic, prop-like palette.

## B — messy underworld stack

- Keep the basic rectangular bundle from [Sketchfab / Vertex cash lowpoly](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/cash-lowpoly-f99e0819ea0348deb83019e9d57a9dcd), then lean its top sheets and bend the corners slightly.
- More character, but less predictable for four-view image-to-3D and harder to reuse as a clean stack.

## C — several bundles as one prop

- Use the grouped silhouette of [Fab / Low Poly Money Pack](https://www.fab.com/listings/24f0ae73-9f9e-47cf-8b0f-da3a5bbd3963) as general inspiration, not copied imagery or mesh. Create a few thick bundles with simple spacing.
- Reads as a pile immediately, but each bundle is less reusable in the game and Tripo may fuse them into one mesh.

Recommendation: approve A for the reusable base. Its separate band, note body, and optional top bill can be rebuilt or separated in Blender after Tripo. Use fictional banknote graphics rather than an exact real currency design.
