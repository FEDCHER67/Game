## Build and validation

- Blender 5.2.1 LTS. Source imported as 4 visible connected components plus one tiny loose artifact; the artifact was omitted from the final model. The source shape was preserved.
- Final model: 6 named mesh objects, 6,162 vertices, 6,460 polygons, 12,157 triangulated faces, 5 materials, no missing face materials.
- Dimensions in Blender coordinates: approximately 1.000 × 0.452 × 0.209. The base sits at Z=0 and the origin is centered in plan. Confirm the desired in-game scale when placing the asset in Unity.
- `SM_Cash_LayerStack`, `SM_Cash_MidBill`, `SM_Cash_TopBill`, `SM_Cash_Band`, `SM_Cash_PrintDark`, and `SM_Cash_PrintPale` can be independently selected.
- Preview cameras, lights and ground are in `COL_Cash_Preview`; only `COL_Cash_Geo` meshes were exported.
- Opened the saved `.blend` and reimported the finished FBX into a clean Blender scene. Mesh count, vertex count, polygon count, object names and 5 material names matched. Reimported bounds differed only by floating-point precision. Validation data: `Working/validation.json`.

## Known limits

- Tripo's `SM_Cash_LayerStack` and `SM_Cash_Band` include open and non-manifold edges (97/135 and 64/64 respectively). They are visually hidden in the rendered asset; use a simple box collider for the pickup rather than a mesh collider made from this render mesh. The top and middle bills are manifold.
- The graphic print is shallow geometry, not a texture. Its five-material FBX exports without a texture dependency. Verify the material appearance in the project's URP shader when importing into Unity.
