# KIDNEY-ASTRA-001 — source provenance and attribution

KIDNEY-ASTRA-001 is a newly modeled stylized pair: **two closed kidneys and one descending tube from each kidney**. The two downloaded Sketchfab models were inspected as visual references. No donor vertices, topology, UVs, material maps, or textures were copied into the final Blender scene or FBX. Both authors are credited because their designs informed the final work.

- **Direct geometry adaptation:** None. No donor mesh object or vertex was exported.

## Revision and final asset contents

- **Created from scratch:** two asymmetric closed bean contours lofted into smooth 3D kidneys; two curved descending tubes with rounded ends; UVs; two matte Principled BSDF materials; preview studio.
- **Previously modeled elements removed:** the large crimson/cobalt central vessel network, central connective bridge, fused lower ureter/support V, and all stand-like geometry from the rejected KIDNEY-ASTRA-001 revision. The original donor bladder, adrenal glands, urethra, and internal kidney anatomy were never included in the final asset.
- **Source textures/materials:** None reused or modified. The final asset uses solid color and roughness values only, with no image textures.
- **3D4SCI:** No 3D4SCI asset, geometry, or texture was used.
