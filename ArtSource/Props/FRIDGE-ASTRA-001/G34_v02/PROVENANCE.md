# FRIDGE / G34 collision revision v02

Created locally in Blender 5.2.1 LTS, background CLI only.

Approved G34 visuals and packed atlas were reused without changing their faces, positions, UVs, palette or smooth flags. Some container meshes were split into logical wall/shelf objects solely to keep each visual to 1–4 simple convex collision parts.

No downloaded assets, paid generation, live Blender control, Unity import, commits or pushes. UCX meshes are local convex prototypes for the future Unity import postprocessor. Visual approval remains with Fedya.
