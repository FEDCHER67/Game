# STAINKIT / G34 collision revision v02

Created locally in Blender 5.2.1 LTS, background CLI only.

Four flat glossy raspberry-jam decals (small/medium/large/smear trail) and seven suspicious cartoon evidence props. All red marks are comic jam-like colour, with no realistic gore. All geometry and colour atlases were authored locally; existing Astra colours/chamfers were used as the style baseline.

No downloaded assets, paid generation, live Blender control, Unity import, commits or pushes. UCX meshes are local convex prototypes for the future Unity import postprocessor. Visual approval remains with Fedya.
