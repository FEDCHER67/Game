# HAMMER / G34 collision revision v02

Created locally in Blender 5.2.1 LTS, background CLI only.

New orthopedic bone mallet with cylindrical stainless head and blue ergonomic medical grip; matching separate osteotome. All geometry and colour atlases were authored locally; existing Astra colours/chamfers were used as the style baseline.

Shape-only reference: https://www.surgicalholdings.co.uk/media/files/SH%20Catalogue%20Hip%20Instruments%20A5%20Mar24%20WEB.pdf#page=8 . No reference assets were downloaded or copied.

No downloaded assets, paid generation, live Blender control, Unity import, commits or pushes. UCX meshes are local convex prototypes for the future Unity import postprocessor. Visual approval remains with Fedya.
