# HAMMER Astra v03

Made locally in Blender background CLI by editing the approved G34 v02 source. No downloads, external assets, or paid generation.

Only the mallet striking head and its UCX proxy are uniformly reduced by 22.5%, anchored at the top to keep the 0.294 m total length. Handle, neck, osteotome, UVs, materials, texture pixels and other UCX parts are unchanged. The atlas is copied byte-for-byte from v02.
