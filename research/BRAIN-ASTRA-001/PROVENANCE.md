# BRAIN-ASTRA-001 provenance

The final cerebral geometry, UV0 mapping, solid-color material, and Blender preview studio were authored procedurally in `Working/build_brain_astra.py` for this project. No third-party brain mesh, topology, texture, animation, or material map was imported, adapted, or redistributed. The two final hemisphere meshes are generated directly from mathematical ovoid surfaces and original winding sulcus paths. The existing heart and kidney `.blend` files were opened read-only to align the organ family's finish and lighting; neither supplied geometry.
